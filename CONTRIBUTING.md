# Contributing

A guide to helping keep this a list of resources actually worth using.

## Scope

The Awesome D&D list is a focused list of resources in various categories that are exclusively focused on D&D and system-agnostic resources.
This is not the [Awesome TTRPG list](https://github.com/Zireael07/awesome-tabletop-rpgs)
or the [Awesome Pathfinder list](https://github.com/skaldarnar/awesome-pf2e).

## Suggest a resource

Guidelines for suggesting a new resource to be added to the list:

- Recommend something you've actually tried. For real. Explain what it helps a DM or player do and why it's worth adding alongside other entries.
- Keep it relevant to D&D or system-agnostic tabletop play.
- Check the list and previous issues and PRs for duplicates and discussion.
- Disclose your connection to the resource. Self-promotion is welcome; as long as it is made clear.

Open a PR with one resource, placed in the appropriate category:

```markdown
- [Resource name](https://example.com) - Short, factual description ending with a period.
```

If you're unsure whether something fits, or want to propose a new category, open an issue first.
Suggestions by issue are also welcome if you aren't sure how to make a pull request.

## AI tools and contributions

Oh boy. This is why I'm going through the trouble of writing this whole thing finally. No guarantee that this section stays static forever. Here goes.

AI-powered/AI-created links are eligible to be added to the list.
Describe any AI features readers will encounter on the requested link in the pull request.
Using AI to build a resource isn't an automatic disqualification, but the bar for inclusion on this list should be more than "it helped my D&D group".

## Help with the existing list

Corrections, better descriptions, and broken-link fixes are welcome as direct PRs.

Resources may be removed if they stop working, become misleading, or no longer offer useful value.
Old resources do not automatically mean they are obsolete; a good site of random tables can age way better than a web app.

## How decisions happen

Maintainers (oh hey, that's just me! at least for now!) make the final call, informed by community feedback.
Meeting these guidelines doesn't guarantee inclusion.
As mentioned in the [Awesome Manifesto](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome/blob/main/awesome.md), "Only awesome is awesome. Only put stuff on the list that you or another contributor can personally recommend."
And since I'm only one person today, I will be doing my best to make judgment calls combined with feedback from the community.
This is volunteer work, so reviews happen when time allows.
Be kind, keep feedback specific, and bring new evidence if you'd like a decision reconsidered.
